About the game

Head out from the hub to chop Starwood or mine Moonstone. You carry the load back in plain sight, so you can see what the rest of the crew has been up to on the way home.

When the black hole collapses, everyone gets a payout. A shared roll picks the next hole and its reward multiplier. Spend your Stardust on better tools, or unlock a ship and go looking for rarer ore.

Features

Feed it together

Every player's delivery grows the same black hole, and everyone shares the payout.

Harvest and haul

Trees, crystals, salvage and meteorite cores, carried back as a visible load.

Every collapse rolls

A shared reel picks the next black hole's rarity and reward multiplier.

Gear up

Spend Stardust at the Orbital Outfitter on tools, suits and companions.

Explore

Unlock a ship and fly to distant islands for rare ore.

Play anywhere

Built for PC, phone, tablet and console controllers.
