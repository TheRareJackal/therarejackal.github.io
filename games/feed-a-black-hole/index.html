The concept
Harvest. Feed. Collapse.

What happens when a whole server feeds one tiny black hole? Chop Starwood, mine Moonstone, pull orbital salvage and drill Meteorite Cores, then carry your load back to the hub and watch the hole grow.

Every load brings the crew closer to collapse. When the black hole finally caves in, everyone claims a reward and a shared cosmic roll reveals the rarity you feed next.

What's inside

Features

Feed it together

Every player's delivery grows the same black hole, and everyone shares the payout.

Harvest and haul

Trees, crystals, salvage and meteorite cores, carried back as a visible load.

Every collapse rolls

A shared reel picks the next black hole's rarity and reward multiplier.

Gear up

Spend Stardust at the Orbital Outfitter on tools, suits and companions.

Explore

Unlock a ship and fly to distant islands for rare ore.

Play anywhere

Built for PC, phone, tablet and console controllers.
