About the game

The Wandfall broke the academy and left its lantern road dark. Your wizard stays put while enemy formations advance, and every spell is a committed sky strike with weight, travel time and a recovery window. Lead your targets, interrupt their casters and let your recruits cover the gaps.

Combine Ember, Rime, Storm, Bloom, Stone and Aether into reactions, bind magic weapons to your allies and restore eight districts one battle at a time.

Features

Six elements

Fifteen baseline reactions and advanced confluences with a third element.

36 magical weapons

Six weapon families to wield yourself or bind to an ally.

Recruit an army

Archers, beasts, healing familiars and summoned defenders.

One upgrade tree

A connected, zoomable tree with free respecs between attempts.

24 encounters

Eight districts, their guardians and a final confrontation.

Endless watches

Seeded challenges and endless Observatory defenses after the campaign.

In development

Work-in-progress captures and concept art. Things will change before launch.
