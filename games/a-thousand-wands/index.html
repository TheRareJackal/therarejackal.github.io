About the game

The academy’s lantern road went dark in the Wandfall. To reopen it, fight through eight districts with a wizard who casts from a fixed position. Spells take time to land. A shot aimed at the front rank can hit empty ground by the time it arrives.

Mix Ember, Rime, Storm, Bloom, Stone, and Aether to set off reactions. Bind a weapon to a recruit and you can keep that part of the line fighting while you aim somewhere else.

Features

Six elements

Fifteen baseline reactions and advanced confluences with a third element.

36 magical weapons

Six weapon families to wield yourself or bind to an ally.

Recruit an army

Archers, beasts, healing familiars and summoned defenders.

One upgrade tree

A connected, zoomable tree with free respecs between attempts.

24 encounters

Eight districts, their guardians and a final confrontation.

Endless watches

Seeded challenges and endless Observatory defenses after the campaign.
