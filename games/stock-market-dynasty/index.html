About the game

The trading desk has candlestick charts, watchlists, and order tickets. All the companies are fictional, but everyone on the server sees the same prices. Two friends can look at the same chart and take opposite sides.

You can trade long or short, set an exit price, and watch what happens when news hits. Profits pay for better equipment at the desk or property in another state.

Features

A real terminal

Candlestick charts, indicators, watchlists and order tickets built for Roblox.

Long and short

Bet on the rise or the fall, with leverage, take-profit and stop-loss.

Living market

A synthetic market of companies that moves with news and momentum.

Desk upgrades

Level up and unlock better tools for your trading desk.

Empire

Use trading profits to buy property, state by state.

Play together

Everyone in the server trades the same market.
