In developmentMusic-driven action roguelite
PC & Mac
The concept
Build your song. Survive each wave.

Nightwinder is a music-driven action roguelite. Choose one of twelve musicians, step into a single large arena and survive twelve waves, ending with a guardian. Your notes attack automatically, in time with the music.

Every choice you make, from new notes and effects to a whole song style, is arranged into a song for you. The Chorus builds over four bars and releases on the downbeat, and between waves you spend Run Gears in the shop and Banked Gears in the Workshop.
