The concept
Keep the song until morning.

A brass automaton has seven turns of its key to keep a grieving child asleep until dawn. Each night her fears come up through the floorboards, and each night it fights them to the rhythm of a lullaby her grandmother never finished.

Nightwinder plays as a survivors-style roguelite where the build is a song: pins on the keeper's 16-step drum fire on the beat, and the ending turns out to be the song you built.

Features
