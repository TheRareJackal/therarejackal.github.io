Nightwinder

Listen for the attack you just added.

In developmentMusic-driven action roguelite
PC & Mac

About the game

Pick a musician and stay alive for twelve waves. Your notes attack automatically in time with the music. Add a note or an effect and it becomes part of the song you hear for the rest of that run.

Listen through four bars for the Chorus to land on the downbeat. Between waves, Run Gears buy changes for the current performance. Banked Gears go back to the Workshop for the next one.

Features

Twelve musicians

Drummer to Organist, each with signature notes and two song styles.

Your build is a song

Choices are arranged automatically into what you hear.

The Chorus

Builds over four bars and releases on the downbeat.

Twelve waves

Escalating enemies, hazards and a guardian at the end.

Shop and Workshop

Spend Run Gears between waves and Banked Gears between runs.

Fresh starts

Unlock wave-5 and wave-9 starts and Remix challenges.
