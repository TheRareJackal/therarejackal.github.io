Meat Vector

A Scrapling makes a pretty good projectile.

In development3D action roguelite
PC & Mac

About the game

Each run starts with a choice of tool. Bellows pushes a crowd away, Undertow pulls it close, and Gravejack launches it. Pick up the others as you move through the site.

The trick is finding a line through a crowd that keeps moving. Throw a Scrapling into the pack, slide through the gap, or take to the wall when the floor fills up. This single-player roguelite is in development for an adult audience.

Features

Grip and throw

Grab a creature and send it through the ones behind it.

Three starter tools

Bellows pushes, Undertow pulls, Gravejack launches.

Momentum chains

Link throws together and carry speed through the swarm.

Slide and wall run

Fast movement built for weaving through crowds.

Three industrial courts

Intake yards, machinery courts and the Choir Engine.

Physical combat

Articulated enemies that react to every hit.
