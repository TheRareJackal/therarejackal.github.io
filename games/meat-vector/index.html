PC & Mac
The concept
The swarm is your arsenal.

Meat Vector is a single-player 3D action roguelite about weaponizing the swarm. Grip creatures, throw them, link their momentum, orbit the dead as shields and turn corpses into ammunition.

Fight through three industrial nightmares, choose a tool for each run and shut down the Choir Engine. It's for adults, and it's still very much in the workshop.

What's inside

Features

01
