The Rare Jackal is a one-person game studio. Design, code, art and music all come from the same desk, so every game ships the way it was meant to.

The focus is games you can sink your teeth into: lane battles, horde defense, spellcraft, rhythm, tycoons and the occasional strange experiment. Every PC game runs on Windows and Mac.
